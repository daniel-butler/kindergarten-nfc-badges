# 🎬 Run of show

[← Back to the README](../README.md) · [Presenter guide](presenter-guide.md) · [Setup guide](setup.md)

About 30 minutes, Thursday 2026-11-19.

## The badges

One cardstock rectangle per kid:

- **Top half:** the clear NFC **special sticker**. Kids can see its antenna.
- **Bottom half:** the **animal sticker**.

The two stickers don't overlap. Kids assume the computer reads the animal; the lesson flips that.

You also bring:

| Badge | What's on it | Registered as |
|---|---|---|
| **Mystery badge #1** | Only an animal sticker (a cat). No NFC. | Nothing (there's nothing to read) |
| **Mystery badge #2** | Only a special sticker. No animal. | A normal card with **badge #1's cat** photographed as its sticker |
| **Trick badges** (3) | Special sticker that "says the wrong thing" | 🎭 Trick: one with the wrong color, one the wrong animal, one both wrong |
| **Golden badge** | Any animal | ⭐ Golden role (stored as `special`) (call it the **golden badge** out loud) |

## Who does what

- **Dad (presenter):** talks, runs the Mac (**⌥** shortcuts) and follows Charlotte around.
- **Charlotte:** carries the mystery basket and scans badges with the **phone** (Zookeeper →
  **Check in**).
- **USB reader** on the Mac: backup scanner.

## The show

| # | Step | On screen | You / Charlotte |
|---|------|-----------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Intro:** "I'm Charlotte's dad, I work at Amazon on security: how people get in and out of the building." | ⌥3 zoo, empty | |
| 2 | **Badges out:** Charlotte brings the mystery basket. | | Hand out one badge per kid |
| 3 | **Everyone scans in.** | ⌥3: each animal walks through the gate, tally grows | Charlotte scans each kid's badge with the phone; follow her around. Reader is backup |
| 4 | **The question:** "Badges on your heads! How did the computer know it was YOUR animal?" | ⌥3, everyone playing | Talk |
| 5 | **Mystery badge #1** (animal only): tap it. Nothing happens. A kid tries it too. Still nothing. | Nothing changes | Tap it on the phone or reader |
| 6 | **Mystery badge #2** (special sticker only): kids predict with thumbs, drumroll, tap. **A cat walks into the zoo.** "How did it know?" | Cat arrives at the gate, confetti | Tap it |
| 7 | **Reveal:** "The computer reads the special sticker, not the animal!" Call and response, then kids find the special sticker on their own badges. | | Talk |
| 8 | **Trick badges:** "What if the special sticker says the wrong thing?" Wrong color, wrong animal, both wrong. Thumbs up or down for each. | Vote card: the stored animal and color, 👍 Let in / 👎 Keep out | Tap each trick badge, then **⌥Y** / **⌥N** (or the phone) |
| 9 | **Finale:** the golden badge is turned away. You allow it, it walks in, **party!** | Goose + padlock → Zoo List scribble → walks in → party | Tap it, **⌥A**, tap it again |
| 10 | **Decorate badges, Q&A.** | Party | |

## Before class

Follow the [pre-class checklist](presenter-guide.md#pre-class-checklist), plus:

- [ ] Mystery badge #2 is registered as the cat, with **badge #1's sticker** photographed.
- [ ] Mystery badge #1 is **not** registered.
- [ ] The three trick badges and the golden badge are registered and work.
- [ ] Rehearse steps 5 and 6 back to back: #1 does nothing, #2 brings the cat.
- [ ] **Reset** right before the kids start.
